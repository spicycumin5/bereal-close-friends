BeReal.
Close Friends

Keep the whole friend list, but give the people who matter most their own lens on the feed, and a week together worth rewatching.

  1. 1

    Onboarding

    An in-app intro, then suggested close friends based on who you RealMoji the most.

  2. 2

    Mutual requests

    You send a request and they accept. Their phone slides in so you can watch it land and get accepted.

  3. 3

    The Close Friends tab

    Not new posts, just a filter on the feed you already have.

  4. 4

    Phone off? Still in the loop

    The lock screen tells you when close friends post: right at BeReal time, and again when someone posts late.

  5. 5

    Your week, every Sunday

    A chronological reel of the circle's BeReals, day by day.

Unofficial concept. Not affiliated with BeReal. Tap a photo to swap cameras; in the recap, tap to skip and hold to pause.
